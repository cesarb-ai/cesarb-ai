 ## The Philosophy: Agency Beyond Chatbots
 
 My background in Philosophy (UC Berkeley) and Psychology (NYU) shapes how I design non-deterministic systems. I think in terms of mental models, boundaries, and agentic intentionality: what an agent knows, what it can’t know, and how it chooses to act under uncertainty. This leads to architectures where AI agents have explicit goals, memory, and affordances—not just prompts. In practice, that means rigorous observability, clear state machines (e.g., LangGraph), and feedback loops that mirror real cognitive processes.

 ## Featured Production Systems
 
 ### Case Study: Autonomous Onboarding Agent (Vision + Web Browsing)
 
 - **Context**: At Coinbase, onboarding internal users onto complex tooling and workflows was consuming a significant amount of high-leverage engineering time.
 - **Role**: I architected and led the development of an autonomous onboarding agent that combines vision (UI understanding) with web-browsing capabilities to navigate real internal tools end-to-end.
 - **Architecture**: Multi-agent LangGraph orchestration, LangSmith-driven observability, tool-augmented browsing, and policy-guarded actions. Integrated with internal services via Kafka, Golang, and Python microservices.
 - **Impact**: The agent guides employees through bespoke workflows, handles edge cases, and escalates when appropriate—**saving hundreds of hours monthly** and standardizing knowledge that used to live only in people’s heads.
 - **Keywords**: AI Agent Building, Enterprise RAG, LangGraph Production, tool-augmented autonomy, observability-first design.
 
 ---
 
 ### Case Study: KB Engine Playground (Knowledge Base Engine)
 
 - **Context**: Teams needed a reliable way to prototype and harden RAG pipelines over heterogeneous, evolving knowledge bases (docs, wikis, tickets, code, internal tools).
 - **Role**: I designed and built the **KB Engine Playground**, a production-ready experimentation surface for knowledge base ingestion, retrieval, and evaluation.
 - **Architecture**: Modular ingestion graph, vector + symbolic retrieval, pluggable rerankers, and LangGraph-based flows for complex multi-hop queries. End-to-end observability via LangSmith to track retrieval quality, hallucination rates, and latency.
 - **Impact**: Enabled rapid iteration on RAG strategies, objective evaluation of retrieval performance, and smoother promotion from “playground” experiments into hardened enterprise RAG services.
 - **Keywords**: Enterprise RAG, Knowledge Base Engine, retrieval evaluation, LangGraph Production, agentic workflows over knowledge.
